Orderinstall.com - Streamlining Telematics Hardware Installation

At OrderInstall.com, we take pride in efficiently managing telematics hardware installation through our network of skilled auto electricians. Our end-to-end solution ensures a seamless experience for clients and auto electricians alike.



Our Network

Over 300 experienced auto electricians across Australia and New Zealand. Comprehensive training, proprietary software, wiring diagrams, and resources provided. Skilled professionals adept at diverse installation scenarios.

OrderInstall.com
Installation Management Process

Order Creation: Clients place orders with vehicle details and requirements.
Auto Electrician Matching: Our system selects the most suitable auto electrician based on location and vehicle details.
Shipping: Telematics hardware and accessories shipped to auto electrician.
Scheduling: Auto electrician schedules installation in collaboration with vehicle contacts.
Process and Technical HelpDesk: Wiring diagrams and support materials provided, immediate technical assistance available.
Installation: Auto electricians perform installation, system conducts tests and notifies of issues.
Verification and Quality Check: Review by OrderInstall Admin ensures adherence to standards.
Provisioning: Verified devices provisioned to client accounts or set to 'Auto-Provision'.
Feedback and Rating: Clients provide feedback for continuous improvement.
Billing and Payment: System generates accurate invoices based on installation and services.
Post-Installation Support: Clients access HelpDesk for queries, issues, prompt resolutions.

Efficiency: Streamlines process, reduces errors.

Quality Assurance: Ensures adherence to standards, encourages feedback.
Convenience: Centralized resources, easy access for auto electricians.
Scalability: Manages multiple installations across diverse locations.




OrderInstall – A SaaS Platform for Managing Field-Based Workflows, Inventory, and Subcontractor Networks

OrderInstall is a flexible SaaS platform designed to streamline how businesses manage product and service orders that require field-based fulfilment by subcontractors. Whether you’re coordinating installations, deliveries, fitments, or service calls across regions, OrderInstall centralises the entire process — from order creation and logistics to field execution and invoicing — while giving businesses access to a qualified, ready-to-go installer network.
 
Core Capabilities
•	Order Lifecycle Management: Create, assign, and track orders for physical products and services requiring on-site action — such as installation, configuration, delivery, or inspection.
•	Inventory Management System (IMS): Manage hardware stock levels, warehouse locations, and product movement across multiple sites. Streamline shipping and logistics workflows to ensure the right parts arrive at the right job, on time.
•	Subcontractor Scheduling & Coordination: Automate job allocation to third-party installers or service agents based on geography, availability, skillset, or other business logic.
•	Instructional Support: Upload job-specific documents, PDFs, videos, or URLs to guide subcontractors through each job confidently.
•	Smart Job Assignment: Match jobs to your own contractors or leverage OrderInstall’s growing marketplace of skilled installers based on criteria such as location and job type.
•	Live Job Status Tracking: Monitor progress in real-time, capture proof of completion, and alert stakeholders of updates as they happen.
•	Customer Communication Hub: Centralise all communications between your internal teams, customers, and subcontractors, with built-in notifications and reminders.
•	Automated Billing Flows: Automatically generate invoices between subcontractors, OrderInstall, and your business — with support for flexible commission models.
•	Status Reporting & Digital Sign-Off: Collect on-site job data including photos, notes, timestamps, and customer signatures.
•	Custom Workflows: Tailor your job flow, approval processes, and integration touchpoints to your specific industry needs.
•	Analytics & Compliance Monitoring: Measure performance across installers, job types, and timelines with dashboards, SLA tracking, and alerts.
 
Tailored for Industries Like
•	Telematics & Fleet Hardware Deployment
•	Automotive Accessories Fitment (e.g. dashcams, GPS, RUC hardware)
•	IoT, EV Chargers, and Solar Panel Installations
•	Home & Appliance Setup Services
•	Commercial Equipment & Infrastructure Fitouts
 
Business Benefits
•	Centralised platform reduces coordination overhead
•	Built-in inventory and logistics visibility reduces shipping errors and delays
•	Improved job turnaround times and service reliability
•	Clear documentation and proof ensures quality and compliance
•	Scale with your business — use your own network or our skilled subcontractor pool
•	Seamless integration with your CRM, ERP, and logistics systems
 
A Platform for Subcontractors Too

OrderInstall isn’t just for businesses — it’s a lead-generating platform for subcontractors. Skilled tradespeople, installers, and technicians can sign up, define their job preferences and service regions, and start receiving verified job offers — with zero paperwork or payment chasing.

Subcontractor Features
•	Create a profile and specify your skills, job types, and service areas
•	Receive job offers matched to your availability and preferences
•	Get clear instructions and digital tools to complete work efficiently
•	Submit proof of completion and receive prompt payment
•	Invoicing and financial tracking handled within the platform
 
Built-in Two-Sided Network Model
•	Businesses gain a reliable, scalable workforce for field execution
•	Subcontractors gain access to quality job opportunities and fast payment
•	OrderInstall acts as the trusted intermediary, powering the logistics, workflows, and billing between both sides
