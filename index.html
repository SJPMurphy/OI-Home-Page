What is OrderInstall?

OrderInstall is a flexible SaaS platform designed to streamline how businesses manage product and service orders that require field-based fulfilment by subcontractors. Everything from order creation and logistics to field execution and invoicing—centralised in one place.

Centralised Workflow

Manage jobs, logistics, and subcontractors in one intuitive dashboard.

Inventory Management

Track, ship, and manage hardware across multiple locations with ease.

Smart Job Assignment

Automate job allocation and match work to the right installer, every time.

Order Lifecycle Management

Subcontractor Scheduling & Smart Matching

Real-time Job Tracking
