Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about OrderInstall.

Who is OrderInstall for?
OrderInstall is ideal for businesses that coordinate field-based services—whether you're managing a national network of subcontractors or running a local operation. It's built for ops managers, dispatch teams, logistics coordinators, and installers alike.

How long does it take to get started?
Most teams are up and running within a day. We offer guided onboarding, including account setup, installer invites, and template configuration—so you can hit the ground running.

Can I use my existing installers?
Absolutely. You can onboard your existing subcontractors and internal teams into the platform—or access our verified installer network to fill coverage gaps.

Does OrderInstall handle installer payments?
Yes. OrderInstall generates automated invoices for subcontractors, allowing your finance team to easily approve and disburse payments through your preferred method.

Can I track inventory across jobs?
Yes. You can manage stock, assign gear to jobs, track shipment status, and monitor inventory levels by location—all within the platform.

Is there a way to prove job completion?
Each job includes photo uploads, timestamped check-ins, digital signatures, and GPS validation—giving you complete transparency and accountability.

How customizable is the platform?
Very. From job templates and forms to user permissions and workflows, we can configure the system to fit your operational model.

Is there an API for integrations?
Yes. OrderInstall includes a robust API that allows you to integrate with CRMs, ERPs, asset platforms, or custom workflows.

What kind of analytics are available?
The dashboard includes insights on job performance, time-to-completion, inventory trends, installer activity, and more. Export reports or connect your BI tool via API.
